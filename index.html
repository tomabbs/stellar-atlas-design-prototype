built by people who have spent their lives discovering, releasing, and championing music. Our labels reflect where we come from; our services and technology are the tools and teams we always wished existed—built to help independent artists and labels do more, reach further, and stay independent.

{{ s.n }} {{ s.k }} {{ s.who }} ↓ {{ s.line }}
01 Stellar Atlas label group Independent Record Label Group

Five independently curated labels and a catalog of 411 albums, sharing the infrastructure to take adventurous music further. Each label signs artists, builds its catalog, and defines its own identity on its own terms, while the group connects them through distribution, promotion, manufacturing, licensing, fulfillment, and a shared network built around independent music.

↗ {{ l.name }} {{ l.cat }} {{ l.desc }}
02 Clandestine label services ↗ Independent Music Services

Founded in 2010, Clandestine Label Services provides the people, expertise, and infrastructure independent labels and artists need to release music. Our team has helped bring more than 1,000 albums to market, providing PR, radio promotion, distribution, fulfillment, manufacturing, licensing, and hands-on label and project management for our own family of labels and a select group of outside clients. Services can stand alone or work together as a complete release team, built around what each record actually needs.

{{ v.name }} {{ v.cat }} {{ v.desc }}
03 Haulix software studio

Founded in 2009, Haulix has spent more than 16 years as a trusted name in digital music promotion, helping the music industry securely distribute new releases to media around the world. Today, we’re building on that foundation with a growing suite of purpose-built software for the people who release, promote, manage, and sell music—from promotion and media monitoring to project management, commerce, and fulfillment.
